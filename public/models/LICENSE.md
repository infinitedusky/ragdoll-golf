# Models

## golfer.glb

- File: `Xbot.glb` from the three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb`, r170), renamed.
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/models/gltf
- Origin: Adobe Mixamo's "X Bot" character (exported by three.js with Khronos glTF Blender I/O).
- Licence: **not CC0.** Mixamo characters are royalty-free to use in personal and commercial projects
  under Adobe's Mixamo terms, but may not be redistributed as stand-alone assets. three.js ships it in its
  examples under that understanding. Fine for this throwaway prototype; the real build should pick a
  CC0 rig (see PROTOTYPE.md) or confirm Adobe's terms cover a public repo.
- Rig: Mixamo, 67 bones named `mixamorig:*`, T-pose bind with identity rotations, faces +Z,
  authored in centimetres under a 0.01 root scale (≈1.8 m tall), feet at y ≈ 0.
